The Legacy

About The RK Residency

Conceived as a masterstroke in commercial modernism, crafted for discerning guests.

Redefining the Contemporary Retail Experience

Established with the vision to deliver a world-class cosmopolitan center, The RK Residency Mall stands as an iconic landmark at the city’s heart. Inspired by classical European colonnades blended with modern minimalist glass atriums, natural skylights flood the 5-tiered promenade.

Beyond shopping, The RK Residency is a vibrant cultural epicenter, hosting art exhibitions, festive galas, runway showcases, and pop-up events year-round. Our LEED Gold-certified facility incorporates solar harvesting, smart water recycling, and sustainable zero-emission waste processing.
